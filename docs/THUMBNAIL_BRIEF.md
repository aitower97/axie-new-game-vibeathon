# Axie Tactics Dice — Thumbnail brief

**Goal:** a legible, eye-catching image that says "Axie + dice + tactics" even at thumbnail size (~300 px wide).

## Composition

- **Title:** "AXIE TACTICS DICE" in large chunky rounded letters (the in-game cover lockup style: solid outline, flat fill, hard drop shadow, no blur). Max 2–3 words on the whole image.
- **Hero:** one or two recognizable 3D Axies (e.g. a Beast and an Aqua, from the game's own 3D renderer) in the foreground, facing each other or the camera.
- **The dice:** a large 3D die beside/between them showing a body-part icon (e.g. the Imp horn) on its visible face. "Body part = die face" must be visible at a glance.
- **Board:** the isometric 3D grid tiles as background, slightly darkened at the far end to keep focus on the hero and die.
- **Feel:** tactical and playful — strong diagonal composition, saturated Axie-style colours (the cover screen's light sky palette), clear contrast between subject and background.

## Do

- Capture with the game's own camera: start a Free match, roll, select a unit, screenshot at 1920×1080 with the action pad hidden or cropped.
- Keep the die and title in the top/centre third; they must survive a crop to 16:9 and 1:1.
- Test the result at 300×170 px.

## Avoid

- Full-UI screenshots (HUD, log, cards, panels).
- More than one line of text besides the title.
- The official Axie Infinity wordmark/logo or any logo we don't have rights to (the repo deliberately removed the "AXIE INFINITY" wordmark for compliance).
- Busy backgrounds or tiny details.

## Reusable material in the repo

- `public/brand/logo.svg` — the game's own logo.
- The Axie Origins VFX in `public/vfx/` (combat effects) for reference art.
- The cover screen (`#/portada`) already shows the Lord and roster in 3D; a clean screenshot of it is the fastest thumbnail base.
